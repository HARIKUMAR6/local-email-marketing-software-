Before you write, a few quick answers

How quickly will I get a reply?

Sales and general enquiries are answered within one business day. Priority support plans receive replies within four hours.

Can I book a walkthrough or a call?

Yes. Choose “Sales” in the enquiry form and mention a preferred time. We will confirm a slot by email.

Which hours is the team available?

Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

Is the map location a VELORA office?

No. The map shows a temporary reference location only. Please contact us first so we can confirm how to meet.

Where can I find product help?

Use the Support option in the enquiry form or email support@velora.io with a description and screenshots where possible.
