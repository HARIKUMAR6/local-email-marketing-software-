Our story

It started with an inbox we didn’t want to open.

In 2019, our founders — a marketer drowning in clunky tools and an engineer tired of shipping noise — asked why sending a good email felt so hard. Campaigns lived in one tool, contacts in another, and results in a spreadsheet nobody trusted.

So they built one calm workspace where campaigns, audiences, automation and reporting work as one. Every release since has followed the same test: does this help someone send something worth reading?

Growth journey

From a two-person idea to a platform teams rely on

and a team that still reads every support message.

  1. 2019 · The idea

    Two founders start VELORA from a shared kitchen table.

  3. 2021 · Automation arrives

  4. 2022 · Going global
